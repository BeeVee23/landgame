Thailand is Southeast Asia's second-largest economy and one of the world's top rice exporters, with more forest cover than many people expect and one of the region's most advanced solar programmes.

Note: The solar+battery figures are a thought experiment generated with the aid of an AI, not a policy recommendation. All solar figures refer to utility-scale, grid-connected systems. Thailand's electricity mix remains dominated by natural gas (~64%) and coal (~15%), with renewables — mostly biomass, plus growing solar and some hydro — making up roughly a fifth of generation. Thailand also imports around 15% of its electricity from Laotian hydropower. The government's draft power development plan targets 51% renewable electricity by 2037. Land use figures from FAO FAOSTAT and CIA World Factbook 2018–2023 estimates; electricity figures from IRENA Thailand Country Profile 2023.
