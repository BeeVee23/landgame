Changelog

How is land actually used? — version history · major = architectural changes · minor = new countries or significant features · patch = bug fixes and small tweaks

v4.8.0 2026 minor
  • 🇪🇬 Egypt added — Arabic/English toggle; Egypt is one of the world's most dramatically divided landscapes: over 92% is hyperarid desert, while a narrow Nile Valley and Delta — just 3.6% of the land — feeds 105 million people. The current solar category features Benban Solar Park near Aswan (~1.8 GW across 37 km²), one of the world's largest solar installations. The world card is unusually optimistic: Egypt's extraordinary sunshine means it could theoretically power all global electricity demand from just 17% of its own land — a patch of the Western Desert — making it the most solar-credible country in the game so far.
v4.7.1 2026 patch
  • Flag images in all games — Chrome on Windows and Linux cannot render flag emoji, displaying them as two-letter codes (DK, FR, etc.) instead. All 15 game files and the hub now use flagcdn.com flag images in place of emoji, rendering correctly across all browsers and operating systems.
  • Browser back button fixed — hitting the browser back button from inside a game now correctly returns to the world map. Root cause: setting iframe.src creates shadow history entries in the iframe's joint session history; the back button was stepping through those before reaching the map's popstate. Fixed by using contentWindow.location.replace() throughout, which navigates without adding history entries. Also fixed iframe.src = '' (which was inadvertently loading index.html into the hidden iframe) to use 'about:blank' instead.
v4.7.0 2026 minor
  • 🇩🇰 Denmark added — Dansk/English toggle; Denmark has the highest share of arable land of any country in the world (>62%), yet most people assume it's a small industrial nation. Golf courses (~0.24%) and utility-scale solar (~0.14%) occupy similarly tiny slivers — in the same order of magnitude as each other. The world card is particularly dramatic: Denmark's cloudy, high-latitude climate means it would need land equivalent to over 6× its entire territory to power the world with solar. Wind is, of course, a very different story — ~55% of Danish electricity already comes from it.
v4.6.0 2026 minor
  • 🇩🇪 Germany added — Deutsch/English toggle; 50% farmland and 30% forest despite its industrial reputation. Golf courses (~0.157%) occupy almost exactly the same land as current utility-scale solar (~0.21%). The disclaimer addresses the German automotive industry's turbulent ICE-to-EV transition: VW, BMW and Mercedes directly employ 780,000 people, EV sales fell 27% in 2024, and VW is cutting 35,000 jobs by 2030 as Chinese rivals gain ground.
v4.6.0 2026 patch

  • Map legend contrast fixed — the country outline is now a clearly muted grey-brown and the land-area-needed circle a vivid orange, matching the legend dots
v4.5 2026 major
  • "What if [country] alone powered the whole world?" — new card revealed after submitting, showing how much land each country's own solar conditions would need to supply all of global electricity demand (~31,000 TWh/yr). Sunnier countries like Australia need under 3% of their own land; cloudier countries like the UK would need over 4× their entire land area, with the overflow visibly spilling into neighbouring countries on the map.
  • Zoomed inset map — alongside the world globe view, a second zoomed-in panel shows the same equivalent-area circle at a scale where it's actually visible, with a kilometre scale bar for a tangible sense of size.
  • Scores normalised to percentages — replaces the old "points out of 800" system, which was actually out of 700/800/900 depending on how many categories a country had. All games now show a single comparable 0–100% accuracy score, with grade thresholds rescaled to match.
