📝 Changelog

Version history for the Land Use Game.

v6.0.0 — Single-file architecture + Canada + Stats

  • Architecture refactor: Migrated from 30 standalone HTML files to a single game.html template + data.js data file. Adding a new country now requires editing one data object, not duplicating 400 lines of HTML.
  • New country: 🇨🇦 Canada — fully sourced from Statistics Canada Land Cover Register 2020 and Natural Resources Canada State of Canada's Forests 2025.
  • Canada context box: Details hydro/nuclear dominance (~75% non-emitting grid) alongside fossil fuel entanglement in government: federal TMX pipeline ownership ($34.2B), IISD-estimated $18.8B fossil fuel subsidy, CAPP lobbying (138 meetings in 2025), CPPIB's $6B+ in new fossil assets and net-zero reversal, and 2025 budget language traceable to oil sands lobbying.
  • Stats page: New stats.html tracks your best scores per country, average accuracy, completion progress, and suggests a random unplayed country.
  • Scoring persistence: Scores are saved to localStorage automatically after each game.
  • Backward-compatible routing: Old hash links (e.g., index.html#ca) redirect to game.html?c=ca.
  • Stub migration: 10 countries included as metadata stubs with official source citations; remaining category data should be ported from original v5 files.

v5.x and earlier

  • Individual HTML files per country, bilingual support, D3 treemaps, orthographic globe visualisations, and world-card scaling comparisons.
  • Scoring normalised to 0–100% with error-tolerance algorithm (15% relative or 1.5 slider steps).
